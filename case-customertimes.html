Anyone can license Claude. Not everyone has a hundred implementations to teach it from.

Customertimes uses Anthropic's Claude as the AI foundation for an accelerated delivery model — but the differentiator is not the model. It is what has been embedded around it: configuration approaches that are known to work, testing protocols for edge cases, and documentation standards that let clients become self-sufficient.

Those patterns come from more than a hundred enterprise implementations spanning Salesforce, SAP, Microsoft and Databricks.

AI across the delivery lifecycle

Claude augments each phase rather than replacing the team. It handles baseline customisation and configuration work, accelerated by accumulated delivery patterns, and reviews every configuration change for consistency against practice refined over hundreds of projects.

Structured business inputs arrive from the customer; AI handles baseline configuration and testing; documentation and training material are generated automatically. Consultant involvement in foundational work drops sharply — and post-implementation support inherits the same institutional memory.
