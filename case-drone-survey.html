The expensive part of a survey is not the flying. It is going back.

Survey teams were making repeat site visits because captures were incomplete, inconsistent, or unusable once processed. Each return trip cost a day and delayed everything downstream.

Orbital combined flight autonomy with a photogrammetry pipeline so a single planned flight produces a complete, measurable 3D model of the site.

Repeatable by design

Because flight plans are stored and repeated, captures from different visits are directly comparable — which turns a survey from a snapshot into a time series of the same site.

The geospatial pipeline delivers outputs in the formats downstream teams already use, so the model becomes part of existing workflows instead of a separate deliverable someone has to convert.
