Sampling tells you a batch was probably fine. Vision tells you which unit was not.

Manual inspection could not keep pace with line speed, so quality was assessed by sampling. Defects reached customers, and the feedback loop back to the line was measured in days.

A vision team and an edge-hardware partner combined to put inspection on the line itself — every unit, as it moves.

Fast enough to matter

Inference runs at the edge in under 40 milliseconds per unit, which is what makes continuous inspection possible without slowing production. Detection accuracy sits at 99.2%, and defects are flagged in time to correct the process rather than to explain it afterwards.

A retraining and monitoring loop keeps the models current as materials, tooling and product variants change — the part that usually decides whether a vision deployment survives its first year.
