Every company has a house style. Almost none of them can scale it.

The client's marketing and internal comms shared a problem: producing consistent material — posts, newsletters, replies — meant a small group of people writing everything, because only they knew how the company was supposed to sound.

Amazinum built a generative system on Google's PaLM 2, connected and fine-tuned through the LangChain framework, then hardened into a production service with Cloud Build, Cloud Functions and Vertex AI.

Teaching a model to sound like a specific company

Sounding on-brand is not a prompt trick. The team prepared large volumes of the client's own newsletters, replies and social copy, then fine-tuned on a Vertex AI pretraining pipeline until the model reproduced the company's register rather than a generic corporate voice.

Around that core sit narrower jobs: acronyms swapped for their full form from a custom company glossary so newcomers can read internal material, social posts generated to a fixed template, and documents translated across formats while keeping their original layout and styling intact.
