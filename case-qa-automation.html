Manual checks scale linearly. Releases do not.

The client shipped frequently enough that ad-hoc manual testing had become the bottleneck and the risk at the same time. Regressions were found by users rather than by the team.

TestFort embedded a dedicated QA team alongside the client's engineers — not a separate vendor throwing results over a wall, but people inside the release process.

From checking to gating

The team replaced repeated manual passes with a maintained automation suite wired into CI, so every build is checked the same way. Exploratory and manual testing was preserved deliberately, but redirected to the areas where human judgement actually finds things.

Clear release gates turned quality from an opinion into a condition: a build either passes or it does not ship.
