A community member built an intelligent fitness app that analyses nine health metrics simultaneously to deliver personalised training, nutrition and recovery — powered by a multi-agent AI coaching engine on Google Gemini.

Company overview

Vitalio — founded by an EU-based entrepreneur and fitness enthusiast — came to the community with an ambition: build the unified wellness product the fragmented fitness market was missing. Not another workout tracker, but a system that combines training, nutrition, sleep and recovery data and uses AI to adapt programmes in real time, autonomously, based on how a person actually performs and lives.

ClientVitalio — EU wellness startup
Duration9+ months (ongoing, pre-launch)
Peak team12 specialists (scaled from 4)

The challenge

Existing fitness solutions operate in silos — no single product connects every wellness dimension, and none use AI to autonomously modify programmes based on real user performance and lifestyle factors. Delivering that meant combining deep AI/ML engineering, mobile product craft, cloud infrastructure and health-domain compliance in one team, then growing that team mid-flight without breaking delivery velocity.

How the community solved it

Multi-agent AI architecture

A layered prompt system with a master-coach persona, feature-specific prompts, and a secondary LLM that validates every output before it reaches the user — keeping coaching advice safe and on-brand.

Nine-metric adaptive engine

The AI simultaneously analyses workouts, nutrition, hydration, sleep, activity, blood biomarkers, supplements, medications and body measurements to continuously re-shape each person's plan.

Conversational-first UX

Natural language replaces rigid forms. Users describe meals in plain words ("I ate borscht without meat"), reschedule workouts and adjust targets through chat — and the AI estimates calories and macros automatically.

AI simulation & testing framework

A custom synthetic-user generator simulates ten weeks of coaching, while three competing LLMs evaluate the AI's decisions to surface errors before real users ever see them.

LLM-agnostic architecture & compliance

The underlying model is swappable via configuration, preventing vendor lock-in. In parallel, a dedicated legal specialist built a 52-document register covering terms of use, health disclaimers and privacy policies.

Progressive team scaling

The engagement grew from a 4-person discovery team to a 10-person delivery squad — and a 12-specialist peak — without disrupting velocity: exactly the elasticity a community of niche companies is built to provide.

The result

  • 9 health metrics analysed simultaneously to power AI-generated, adaptive programmes.
  • 1,500+ exercises in the database, each with video demonstrations.
  • 52 legal documents across three compliance phases, pre-launch essentials complete.
  • 12 specialists at peak capacity, scaled progressively from an initial team of 4.
  • 45% faster time-to-market · 30% lower infrastructure cost · 4.8/5 average app rating.
"One brief to the community became a full AI-native product team — discovery, architecture, backend, mobile, QA, DevOps and compliance, one backlog — that scaled with us instead of slowing us down." — Andrii M., Founder, Vitalio
