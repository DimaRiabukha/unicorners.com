Sellers do not lack data. They lack a reason to act on it.

The client operates a platform where retailers and brands watch their own performance and their competitors' at once. The raw material — prices, availability, sales curves across categories — was already there. What was missing was the layer that turns it into a decision.

Amazinum's data scientists built and evaluated competing model versions autonomously, reworking the evaluation pipeline in Kubeflow so improvements could be measured rather than argued about.

Four questions the platform now answers

The work concentrated on the things a seller acts on weekly: how competitor and retailer prices are moving, which products are drifting out of stock, where advertising spend will actually return, and how the supply chain should respond.

Because the platform runs on GCP, the team worked closely with BigQuery and BigTable to keep queries over large historical windows fast enough for interactive analysis rather than overnight reporting.
