Most fraud systems work. The question is what they cost you in abandoned checkouts.

The client could reduce fraud by adding friction — more checks, more challenges, more abandoned carts. What they wanted was the reduction without the cost.

Kordis AI built a scoring engine that evaluates every transaction inline, fast enough that legitimate customers never notice it happened.

Tuned against the real trade-off

The model was tuned explicitly against the balance between losses prevented and good transactions blocked, rather than against raw detection rate — a distinction that changes which model you ship.

A rules layer sits alongside the model for cases where policy, not probability, should decide. Monitoring tracks drift, because fraud patterns change faster than most models are retrained.
