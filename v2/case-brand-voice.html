How it came together

Teaching a model to sound like a specific company

Sounding on-brand is not a prompt trick. The team prepared large volumes of the client's own newsletters, replies and social copy, then fine-tuned on a Vertex AI pretraining pipeline until the model reproduced the company's register rather than a generic corporate voice. Around that core sit narrower jobs: acronyms swapped for their full form from a custom company glossary so newcomers can read internal material, social posts generated to a fixed template, and documents translated across formats while keeping their original layout and styling intact.
