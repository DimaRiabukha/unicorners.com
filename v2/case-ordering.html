How it came together

Knowing when not to trust the model

The interesting engineering is not the extraction — it is the doubt. The team implemented several methods for scoring confidence, both for the recognised order state and for each extracted entity. When confidence drops below threshold, the system triggers a fallback to human staff instead of committing a guess to someone's order. A prompt-engineering framework sits around the model to keep interpretation stable across the wide range of ways customers actually speak.
