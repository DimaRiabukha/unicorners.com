What we do

We build AI—and make AI work better. Some companies need to bring AI into a product. Others already use AI and need it to perform reliably. Unicorners combines expertise across three areas: AI development, deep-tech engineering and specialized quality assurance.

AI solutions

From the first use case to a working product, we design, build and integrate AI systems around real business needs—including intelligent product features, automation and custom AI applications.

Deep-tech projects

AI quality assurance

When AI is already part of your product, we test how it behaves in practice—across models, data, integrations and user journeys. We uncover hallucinations, edge cases, reliability gaps, performance issues and usability risks, then turn our findings into clear improvements.
